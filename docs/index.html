How it works

A classic NLP pipeline — trained in Python, executed in your browser.

1

Email

Subject and body are combined into one text.

2

Text processing

Lowercase, mask URLs and numbers, drop punctuation and stopwords.

3

TF-IDF

Words and word pairs become weighted numeric features.

4

Naive Bayes

The trained model scores spam vs. safe likelihood.

5

Prediction

A verdict, a confidence score and the words behind it.

Model information

Measured on a held-out 20% test split.
